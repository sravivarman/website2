Power electronics is the technology that enables efficient conversion and control of electrical power. This field forms the backbone of modern electrical systems, from renewable energy to electric vehicles.

## What is Power Electronics?

Power electronics involves the use of semiconductor devices to convert and control electrical power. The primary goal is to achieve efficient power conversion with minimal losses.

## Key Semiconductor Devices

### Diodes
The simplest power electronic device, allowing current flow in one direction only. Used in rectifiers and freewheeling applications.

### MOSFETs
Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistors are widely used in low to medium power applications due to their fast switching speeds.

### IGBTs
Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistors combine the advantages of MOSFETs and BJTs, making them ideal for medium to high power applications.

### SiC and GaN Devices
Wide bandgap semiconductors like Silicon Carbide (SiC) and Gallium Nitride (GaN) are revolutionizing power electronics with higher efficiency and power density.

## Basic Converter Topologies

- **Buck Converter** - Steps down voltage
- **Boost Converter** - Steps up voltage
- **Buck-Boost Converter** - Can step up or down
- **Full-Bridge Inverter** - DC to AC conversion

## Applications

Power electronics finds applications in virtually every sector of electrical engineering, from smartphone chargers to grid-scale energy storage systems.
